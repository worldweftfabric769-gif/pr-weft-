What we do

Weaving and knitting

Air-jet, water-jet and rapier looms for filament wovens, circular knitting for jersey and lycra qualities. Greige is woven to our own specification, not bought loose from the market.

Dyeing and finishing

Piece dyeing, printing and finishing through partner process houses we audit and book capacity with, so lab dips and bulk stay on the same recipe.

Export

Roll-wise packing, full document set and the Incoterm you work on — EXW through to CIF, with DDP quoted on request. And of course, your agent can come for inspection before dispatch.

Private label development

Send a swatch or a tech pack. We match construction, weight and shade, and hold the quality for repeat orders.

Logistics

Surat sits within a day's road run of three container ports, so we can switch gateway if space or rates move against a sailing.

Sea

Air

Documents

Commercial invoice, roll-wise packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, certificate of origin, and inspection report. Preferential certificates issued where a trade agreement applies.

How an order runs

  1. Enquiry

    You send quality, weight, width, quantity and destination port. We reply with a quotation and available shades within two working days.

  2. Sampling

    Header cuts and lab dips go out by courier. Bulk is only booked against your written approval.

  3. Proforma invoice

    Price, Incoterm, payment terms, lead time and packing are fixed in writing before production.

  4. Production

    Weaving, dyeing and finishing run to the approved recipe. You get a weekly status line with metres completed.

  5. Inspection

    4-point inspection on the full lot, with the report and shade continuity cards shared before dispatch.

  6. Dispatch and documents

    Stuffing photographs on loading, scanned documents the same day, originals couriered or sent through the bank.

Quality and certification

Fabric is checked at four points, not once at the end. Anything outside tolerance is held back before it reaches packing.

Incoming yarn

Count, lustre and dye affinity verified against the supplier's lot certificate. Rejected lots are returned, not blended in.

On the loom

Hourly pick and reed checks. Weaving faults are marked on the roll as they occur so they are traceable later.

After dyeing and finishing

Shade against the approved lab dip under D65 and TL84, plus GSM, width and hand feel on every batch.

Before shipment

Full-lot 4-point inspection, roll-wise metrage audit and a shade continuity card for every roll in the shipment.
